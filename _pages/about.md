I am a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Physics and Astronomy](https://www.physics.upenn.edu/) at the University of Pennsylvania. Before Penn, I studied physics and mathematics, with a minor in visual arts, at Johns Hopkins University.

Research
------
My research interests are in generalized symmetries and quantum gravity. Previously, I worked on accretion disks around black holes and black hole binary systems.
