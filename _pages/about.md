I am a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Physics and Astronomy](https://www.physics.upenn.edu/) at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Prof. Jonathan J. Heckman. Before Penn, I studied physics and mathematics, with a minor in visual arts, at Johns Hopkins University.

Research
------
My current projects are on generalized symmetries in liquid crystals and on an effective field theory for the near-horizon region of the black D0-brane. As an undergraduate, I worked on simulations of accretion disks around black holes and binary systems.
